Why: stage B2 builds the club's days. Handoffs 3, 5 and 6 drew most of it; these are the states still without a frame, and one that the founder's later decisions changed. A new member no longer makes everyone confirm a new week: since the founder's answers to FQ125 to FQ127, every member already in the week keeps exactly their days, nobody else is asked anything, and the new member picks from the free days, the first of them that weekday in the week after they confirm (FQ130). When a fourth member joins three who hold all six days, each of the three keeps their first day from Monday, their other day is freed at once, and that weekday's days already announced from the next Monday on are cancelled (the founder's "Free a day now", FQ131). Handoff 6's G04-change draws the earlier rule.
