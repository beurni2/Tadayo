A. Approving a proposed member (handoff 4's G03-approve, handoff 8's frame 10 G03-approve-failed; book p30; spec 04 and 32; T7, T15, T33)

1. G03-approve-two: Maya decides on one revision of Kitchen table that proposes two people: Leila, proposed by Ben, and Nina, proposed by Alex. Every existing member approves the whole roster at once; the server has no way to decide one candidate alone. Built today (FQ144's default): one block per candidate (the 68-point avatar with the initial, the name in personDisplay, "Proposed by {name}"), 16 apart in the revision's order, under "Invite Leila and Nina?", with "To Kitchen table · 5 current members" (the members, never the candidates), the body and boundary naming both ("Leila and Nina must accept the exact roster. …", "Leila and Nina will see only drops shared after joining."), the shield's note, and "Approve Leila and Nina" with Decline. Draw it, or say how two candidates should show.
2. G03-approve-unavailable: the revision ended, never existed, or Maya can no longer see it; the server says the same for all three (T14). Handoff 8's approval.unavailable: "This invitation is no longer available." Built today: those words as the screen's title, alone, with the system's Back to Members (nothing of the roster). Say whether it needs a line or an action, and which.
3. G03-approve-reviewing: Maya tapped Review members on handoff 8's "Review the updated roster" alert, and the app is reading the members again. Handoff 8 says to use the proposed members' loading form ("Loading proposed members…"). Built today: handoff 8's frame 11 loading anatomy under the "New member" bar.
4. G03-approve-review-failed: that read got no answer. Built today: frame 11's failure form with "Couldn’t load proposed members.", "Check your connection and try again." and Retry (hint "Try loading proposed members again.").
5. G05-members-with-proposed: Kitchen table's Members (handoff 1's G05-members, with handoff 4's footer), Maya the viewer, Ben, Maya, Alex, Sam and Debby with one day each, and Leila's proposed row after them (handoff 1: "Leila, Proposed by Ben, Proposed"; the brand sheet's proposed member row). Maya hasn't decided, so the row opens G03-approve. Built today: the proposed row as the invitation's roster draws it (handoff 9's invitationRosterTag), a button with no chevron, no hint and no other mark; the footer counts the five members. Say how the row shows that it opens something, and what VoiceOver reads (today: "Leila. Proposed by Ben. Proposed.", as a button).
6. G05-members-with-proposed-decided: the same after Maya approved (or declined: they must look the same). Built today (FQ143's default): the row opens nothing and says nothing of Maya's decision, so the list looks the same to a member who approved, one who declined and one never asked. Draw it, or say what a decided member's row opens, never saying anyone else's decision.
