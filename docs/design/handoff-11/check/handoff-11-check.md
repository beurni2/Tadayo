## The answer

**Claude Design did what the brief asked.** All 9 frames came, titled and in the brief's order, and every written question has an answer. What matters most:
- **Nothing is said that the sources keep back, though a small club can still work out who removed someone.**
    - Only the person who invited someone gets the removal: on the setup list, the rows of the caller's own candidates; in Members, a separate More on the caller's own candidate's row, never on another member's candidate and never on a member's row.
    - Nobody is told who removed whom. The removed person's link answers as a revoked one (`G02-revoked`, no name, FQ59); an account that had accepted sees `G03-declined`; the others see the existing `G02-changed` and "Review the updated roster". No new words for anyone but the remover.
    - **What the design doesn't promise,** in its own words (`IMPLEMENTATION.md` section 6): "This does not justify a promise that small-group inference is impossible; the product simply does not identify who removed whom." Only the proposer can remove someone (FQ148), and every member sees "Proposed by {name}" on the row (`G05-members-with-proposed`). So when one person drops out of a carried revision while their proposer is still a member, everyone reviewing "Review the updated roster" can tell who removed them, unless a decline, the proposer leaving (FQ121) or FQ120 could have ended it instead. That follows from FQ148 itself; the founder should see it, and the lane doesn't settle it (the PR says so).
    - The confirmation never says anything of the removed person's answers: its body is the same whatever the link's state, and its last paragraph, about anyone else still invited, follows the roster, not the target's answer.
    - None of the 24 new strings has an inferred pronoun or "Sent"; every apostrophe is typographic.
- **The brand is untouched.** The tokens are revision 1.1, byte for byte. The wordmarks, fonts and licence are handoff 10's files, byte for byte. No photograph.
- **Every written question is answered** (section 3): where the removal lives and on which rows, its words, what the list shows after it, its two failure lines, where a later addition's proposer removes them, what the other audiences see, Revoke link's body, and a busy form.

**Where two deliveries disagree** (section 4): frame 5 draws Ben's own candidate's row in Members with no chevron and a separate More, and says no whole-row navigation is added for Ben, though handoff 8's `returnPath`, which routes that row to the person's invitation page, was in what Claude Design read. Since #146 (7c, merged after the `main` Claude Design read) the row opens that page, with frame 5-of-handoff-10's chevron. The later PR keeps the row's route, the reading that loses no way back to the page, and adds the More beside it, marked built from spec; the next brief asks how the two sit together (`docs/design/handoff-7/ios-lane-needs.md`, item 81), and the PR flags it for the founder.

**Where the server and the delivery differ** (section 5): handoff 11 asks for an idempotency key and a roster-version guard on the removal; the api lane's `removeCandidate` takes neither, by design (the candidate's state makes it idempotent, and it names the candidate by id), and nothing in the frames depends on either. And the server answers a candidate something else already ended as removed, so the app would say "Nina’s invitation was removed." where the delivery allows that line only on a committed or replayed removal: a known difference the next brief is asked about. The api lane was told the three points and confirmed them at 04:59 UTC.

**Two more things the delivery doesn't settle,** for the next brief beside frame 5's route (`docs/design/handoff-7/ios-lane-needs.md`, items 82 and 83): the invitation page stays link-only, as handoff 11 says (section 4); and the success line on a removal nobody committed (section 5).

**Nothing blocks the build.** Section 6 lists what the later PR builds and which built states change.

## 5. The server

The api lane's PR #145 (`removeCandidate`, `DELETE /v1/candidates/{candidate_id}`, `docs/plans/api-b2-unpropose.md` section 2 on that branch) and handoff 11's section 10 agree on who (the candidate's proposer, an active member; anyone else NOT_FOUND, T14), on which candidates (any open one, accepted or not; a joined one is NOT_FOUND), on the links (all stop at once), on the open revision (a server-made revision without the removed person, everyone else approving and accepting again, the proposer's approval standing, FQ60 and FQ121; or the end when nobody else is open), and on a retry after a lost answer (answered as removed again). They differ in two places, neither of which a frame depends on, and the server answers one case the delivery's words don't cover:
- **No idempotency key.** Handoff 11 asks for one; `removeCandidate` takes none, because repeating it returns the same answer (as `revokeInvitation` does), so a retry can't commit twice (T19 is about commits a retry could repeat).
- **No roster-version guard.** Handoff 11 asks for "a current-roster concurrency guard"; `removeCandidate` names the candidate by id, takes the club's row lock first (as every roster command does), and answers NOT_FOUND for a candidate who joined meanwhile, so the confirmation can't land on another person. What can be stale is the alert's last paragraph, chosen from the roster as last read; the later PR reads Members or the list again after any answer.

- **A candidate already ended is answered as removed.** A removal of a candidate something else ended a moment before (a decline, FQ120, FQ121) gets the same answer as a removal, and the app can't tell the two apart. So it would say "Nina’s invitation was removed." where the delivery says "The named removal success announcement requires a committed-command acknowledgment or its successful idempotent replay" (`IMPLEMENTATION.md` section 4; `handoff11.setupSuccess.trigger`). The line names nobody else's action and is true of what the remover sees (Nina's invitation has ended), but it departs from the delivery's contract: a known difference (`design/reference/INVENTORY.md`, "Handoff 11"), built as the server answers unless the next brief or the founder says otherwise (item 83).

The api lane was told these three points and confirmed each at 04:59 UTC: no key, since a repeat answers the same; no roster guard, since a candidate who joined answers NOT_FOUND, so the app reads again after every answer; and an already-ended candidate answered as removed, naming nobody.
