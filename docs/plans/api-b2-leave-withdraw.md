# B2: Leaving with "Withdraw my contributions" withdraws them (`api/b2-leave-withdraw`)

The outside audit's finding A3 (2026-11-24, High; live on `main` since #116 merged `createDrop` at `3f1de6b`): `leaveClub` takes the person's choice, stores it on the membership and answers success, and nothing is withdrawn. The file said so (`packages/domain/src/clubs/leave-club.ts`: "Withdrawing the leaver's drops is B6's: the choice is recorded here."). The read predicate has no term for the choice, so a photo its author asked to take back opens for the club at 7 AM and stays in its Memories (spec 22, T26; T23, "denials beat grants"). The audit reproduced it against Postgres: Ben adds a photo, leaves with `withdraw`, and Home at 07:00 lists Ben's photo for Ana and for Cleo. The same gap covers the leaver's live reservations, which stay live until they lapse. The founder's order (relayed by the coordinator at 10:02 UTC on 2026-11-24): A3 first among the api lane's audit items, after #118's A6.

Sources: spec 22 (leaving: "Keep my contributions in the club or Withdraw my contributions"; withdraw "removes that authored drop from all Tadayo views and invalidates highlights, reactions, comments, favourites and widget candidates. After reveal it does not refill a closed occurrence"), spec 11 and 31 (slots), T3, T18 ("a withdrawal before the reveal releases it ... No phantom slots"), T22, T23, T24, T26, FQ63, FQ111, FQ112; the contract's `LeaveClubRequest`, `Contributions` and `RemovedDrop` (`packages/contracts/src/schemas/clubs.ts`, `after.ts`); the schema's `app.drop_removals` (reason `leave`), `app.drops`' guards, `app.highlights`' guard and the deferred `drops_removal_matches` and `drops_ended_unhighlighted` (`db/migrations/0006_contributions.sql`, `0008_safety.sql`).

## 0. The readings

1. **Withdraw in the leave's own transaction.** Of the audit's three fixes (withdraw in the transaction, a term in the read predicate, or refuse `withdraw` until it exists), this is the one the contract already promises ("Withdrawing can't be undone"), the schema already holds (`drop_removals.reason = 'leave'`, the drop `withdrawn`), and every read already honours: a withdrawn drop is absent from every route, Home, the day, Memories and the media route (T24; `packages/domain/src/authorization/read.ts` `mayRead`). Nothing new is read anywhere; the leave writes what a withdrawal is.
2. **Which drops: every drop of the leaving membership in the club, active or held, revealed or not.** "My contributions" names no day, so a drop in Memories goes too (spec 22: "from all Tadayo views"). That includes the leaver's own drops on their own days that haven't opened, which are held (FQ63); other people's drops on those days stay as FQ63 has them, hidden and with their authors. A held drop (T29) is withdrawn too: holding it isn't the author's choice, withdrawing it is.
3. **Only the leaving membership's drops (FQ142, reserved through the coordinator on 2026-11-24).** An account that left the club earlier, keeping its drops, and rejoined has a second interval; its earlier interval's kept drops were kept by an explicit choice. No source says whether a later "withdraw" reaches them. Default: no, the leaving interval's drops only. Withdrawal can't be undone, and the earlier kept drops can still be withdrawn from the author's own contribution route (spec 22), so this is the reading that can still be widened. Recorded in `docs/QUESTIONS.md` and flagged in the PR.
4. **A withdrawal before the drop's reveal gives its slot back; after it, no slot reopens** (T18; the contract's `RemovedDrop`; spec 22: "After reveal it does not refill a closed occurrence"). Before the reveal the counter's `committed_total`, and `committed_external` for a link, go down by one. It changes nothing anyone sees, since the leaver can add nothing more, but the counters stay the drops' (no phantom slots).
5. **The leaver's highlight on a withdrawn drop is cleared** (T22, spec 22: "invalidates highlights"): `drop_id` null, `version` one more, `updated_at` the leave's instant. The database requires it (`drops_ended_unhighlighted`), and from the reveal on allows it only with the drop's end written first in the same transaction (`highlights_guard`), which is the order below. No route sets a highlight yet; the step is there for when one does (`commitDrop` in the tests can write one).
6. **A drop's pending replacement goes with it** (`pending_upload_id` null): the schema allows one only on an active or held drop. No route makes one yet; the upload then lapses (spec 33).
7. **Every leave gives back the leaver's live reservations, whatever the choice.** A reservation isn't a contribution: it's a slot held while a photo prepares, and nobody can use it once the membership has ended (createDrop answers a former member NOT_FOUND). Leaving it live until it lapses is the phantom slot T18 forbids. Under each counter's lock the lapsed ones are settled first (`settleLapsed`: `expired` at their own `expires_at`), then the rest are `released` at the leave's instant and taken off the counts, as `releaseReservation` does (`packages/domain/src/drops/counter.ts` `freeSlot`). An upload still preparing for a released slot is handled as it already is after a release.
8. **What this slice doesn't build, and why it is still closed.**
   - A withdrawn drop's conversation, reactions and favourites: no route makes any of them yet (B5), and each is read through the drop's predicate (T63), which a withdrawn drop fails. The leaver's own messages on other drops follow FQ111's default when conversations are built.
   - The purge of a withdrawn drop's caption and media objects, and its tombstone (T27, T28): that is the durable job B6 builds for every withdrawal and removal. Until then the bytes stay in Storage, unreachable: media comes only through the media route (T12), which reads the predicate.
   - The author's later withdrawal of kept drops (`removeDrop`, spec 22's contribution route): B6.

## 1. The command, step by step

`leaveClub` (`packages/domain/src/clubs/leave-club.ts`) keeps its steps 1 to 5 and 6 to 9. A new step comes after step 5, the membership's end, at the same instant `at` (the clock's sample after the club's row, T1):

**5a. The leaver's contributions.**

1. The counters the leaving interval has in the club with an active or held drop or a live reservation, in occurrence id order, each `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (the lock `lockCounter` takes; no row is made). The order is TECH-DESIGN section 5's: the club's row is held `FOR UPDATE` since step 3, and the membership row since step 5's update; then the counters, then their reservations and drops. Every command that adds to a day, gives back a slot or commits takes the club's row `FOR SHARE` first (`packages/domain/src/drops/reserve.ts` `lockDay`, `release.ts`, `commit.ts`), so none is in flight while the leave holds it, and none starts until it commits; a preparation in the worker takes a counter only after the upload, as `releaseReservation` already meets it.
2. Per counter: its lapsed reservations settled (`settleLapsed` at `at`), then each live reservation released at `at` (`freeSlot`), each row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` first, as `releaseReservation` takes it.
3. If the choice is `withdraw`, per counter, its drops of the leaving interval that are active or held, `FOR UPDATE`, in id order. For each:
   - the removal: `app.drop_removals (drop_id, reason 'leave', actor_interval_id the leaving interval, removed_at at)`;
   - the drop: `state 'withdrawn'`, `ended_at at`, `updated_at at`, `pending_upload_id` null (its version doesn't move: the content didn't change);
   - the leaver's highlight on it, if any: cleared, as section 0.5;
   - before its day's reveal (`at < reveal_at`), its slot given back: `committed_total - 1`, and `committed_external - 1` for a link.
4. The database checks the rest at commit: each withdrawn drop has its one removal and the state its reason gives (`drops_removal_matches`), and no highlight is left on it (`drops_ended_unhighlighted`).

A drop's `updated_at` must move with its state (`drops_guard`). The leave's instant is after every earlier change of the drop: each was made in a transaction that held the club's row `FOR SHARE`, which ended before the leave took it `FOR UPDATE` and sampled the clock.

The answer is unchanged (`LeftClub`: the club, `left_at`, the choice), and so is the receipt: a retry under the key answers it again and withdraws nothing more; a refused leave withdraws nothing (it rolls back).

## 2. Tests first (seen failing on `main` before the fix)

`tests/src/db/leave-withdraw.test.ts`, against Postgres as `tadayo_app`, with the world of `tests/src/db/world.ts` (Ana's Saturday; Ben and Cleo) and `leaveClub` itself:

1. **The audit's case:** Ben's photo and link added to Ana's Saturday before the reveal; Ben leaves with `withdraw`. Each drop is `withdrawn` at `left_at` with one removal, reason `leave`, by Ben's interval; Ben's counter is back to nothing committed; at 07:00 Home lists neither for Ana or Cleo, and the predicate gives neither caption nor media to anyone (`mayReadDrop`); Cleo's own drop is untouched and listed.
2. **After the reveal:** Ben's drop in the opened day, withdrawn by a leave at 09:00: gone from Ana's and Cleo's Home and from the predicate, and Ben's counter as it was (no slot reopens).
3. **Keep:** the same drops stay active, and Ana and Cleo still see them after the reveal, as today.
4. **Reservations, either way:** Ben's live reservation released at `left_at` and off the counts; a lapsed one settled as `expired` at its own `expires_at`, never `released`; a consumed one left as it was.
5. **A held drop** (T29) is withdrawn too; a highlight on a withdrawn drop is cleared with its version one more; a drop's pending replacement is let go.
6. **The leaver's own day:** Ana leaves with `withdraw` before her Saturday opens: Ana's own drops there are withdrawn; Ben's drop for Ana's day stays `active` (held with the day, FQ63).
7. **Nobody else's, nowhere else:** another member's drops, and Ben's drops in another club, are untouched; FQ142: Ben's drop kept by an earlier interval in the same club stays active after the later interval leaves with `withdraw`.
8. **The receipt:** the same leave again under its key answers the first answer and changes nothing; a refused leave (another account's key reuse, a former member) withdraws nothing.

The privacy matrix (`tests/src/privacy/leave.ts`, run by `matrix.test.ts`, whose last phase already makes every role leave with `keep` or `withdraw` in alternate states): a new judge, `contributionProblems`, from the drops', removals', reservations' and counters' rows before and after the leave: with `withdraw`, each of the leaver's active or held drops in the club is `withdrawn` at the instant with one `leave` removal by the leaver, and those before their reveal are off the counter; with `keep`, every drop as it was; either way every live reservation of the leaver's in the club ended (`released` at the instant, or `expired` at its own `expires_at`), and no other interval's drop, reservation or counter changed. Its unit tests in `tests/src/privacy/leave.test.ts` show it catches each of those wrong.

Deliberate breakages at the PR's last head, each caught by the test it names: the withdrawal skipped; only revealed or only unrevealed drops withdrawn; a held drop left; the removal's reason another; the slot given back after the reveal, or not before it; the highlight left; reservations left live on `keep` or on `withdraw`; a lapsed reservation released instead of expired; another interval's drops withdrawn; the earlier interval's kept drops withdrawn.
