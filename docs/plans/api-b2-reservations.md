# B2: Reservations and the allowance counters (`api/b2-reservations`)

`POST /v1/occurrences/{occurrence_id}/reservations` and `DELETE /v1/reservations/{reservation_id}`, the contract's `createReservation` and `releaseReservation` (spec 06, 11, 31, 32; addendum 2; T1, T3, T4, T9, T14, T15, T17, T18, T19, T55; Q09, Q10, Q12; ND01, ND02, ND04; FQ50, FQ63, FQ112). `docs/MILESTONES.md` gives B2's api "Reservations and counters, with the allowance transaction by role (addendum 2; T17) (exercised with test uploads), the cutoff under the lock, idempotency", the last api item of B2.

## 0. The split, and the readings

1. **This PR builds the two reservation routes and the counter's transaction, nothing else.**
   - `createUpload`, `completeUpload` and `createDrop` are B3's: the commit needs a ready upload or a resolved source, which only B3's pipeline and `sources/resolve` make (`docs/MILESTONES.md`, B3). `getOwnDay` answers the author's own drops in their author form, which comes with the author preview (B3, B4).
   - "Exercised with test uploads": the Postgres tests drive the counter through the reservation routes' commands, and the world's `commitDrop` (`tests/src/db/world.ts`), which consumes a reservation and counts a commit as `createDrop` will, supplies the committed drops.
   - The pieces B3's commit reuses are built here and used here: the role's limits and the order of the refusals (`limitRefusal`), the counter's lock (`lockCounter`) and its lapsed reservations settled under it (`settleLapsed`), and who may add to a day at all (`addTargetOutcome`). Nothing is built for B3 alone.
2. **The role comes from the two intervals, never from the request.** The caller's active membership interval in the day's club is the star's exactly when it is the occurrence's `star_interval_id` (T17; addendum 2). `CreateReservationRequest` is a strict object holding only `media_class`, so a role field is refused as INVALID_REQUEST (new-direction plan, section 9, check 1). The counter row's limits are written once by its BEFORE INSERT trigger from the same two intervals (`db/migrations/0010_allowance_by_role.sql`); the command reads them back and throws if they ever disagree with its own reading, which would mean a bug, never a refusal.
3. **A lapsed reservation is settled under the counter's lock, by the command that holds it.** The counter's `reserved_total` and `reserved_external` count live reservations. A reservation lapses at its `expires_at` (T18) with nothing to run: before any command reads or changes a counter, `settleLapsed`, run under the lock `lockCounter` takes, marks that counter's live reservations whose `expires_at` has come (`expires_at <= at`, the clock's sample after the locks) `expired`, with `ended_at` their `expires_at`, and takes them off the counts, in one statement. So a lapsed slot counts for nothing from its `expires_at` on, as `createDrop`'s description says ("A reservation that has lapsed counts as none"), with no worker (T1's spirit: a stopped worker changes no answer). It is the only writer of the `expired` state; a worker job that purges lapsed uploads (B3) must use it. So the counter's `reserved_total` and `reserved_external` are true only under its lock, just after `settleLapsed`: a read that takes no lock (the caller's own allowance in `getOwnDay`, B3 and B4) counts the live reservations whose `expires_at` is after its own clock sample, never the counter's reserved counts as they stand (section 8; round 1's R3).
4. **A reservation holds its slot for 30 minutes, never past the day's reveal: `expires_at = min(at + 30 min, reveal_at)`.** No source gives the duration (spec 11: "an expiring reservation while an upload is preparing"). It is an engineering choice, recorded in the PR's decisions, not a product question: a reservation is the caller's own, so its length only ever affects the caller's own next slot. It covers a 20 MiB upload on a slow network and its preparation; a commit after it lapsed still takes a free slot (`createDrop`'s description), and one that finds none is refused with the caller's allowance and the person's next day, and the work stays in Drafts (T4). The cap at the reveal is also the database's guard (`app.reservations_guard`). Its cost, near the cutoff: an app killed after it reserved, before it used or released the slot, leaves the slot held until it lapses, so a caller whose allowance that slot fills can't add to the day again before the reveal, at most 30 minutes from the reservation (round 1's R2). The app keeps each reservation's id and Idempotency-Key until it uses or releases it, so a retry finds the same slot (`docs/PROGRESS.md`, the needs from the ios lane).
5. **A day whose star has left, or asked to delete their account, before its reveal is held (FQ63), and a reservation in it is MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED with no other field.**
   - The contract gives `createReservation` MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED for "a membership changed since the action started: the person's, so `target`, the day asked for, must be chosen again (T4, spec 32)". The person whose membership can change under a reservation is the day's star: the caller's own leaving makes the club nothing to them (NOT_FOUND, T14), and a request names no interval of the caller's.
   - It carries no `target`: `OccurrencePhase` gives a held day no stage before B6 ("gets its stage with leaving, in B6"), and the app already holds the day it asked for. B6, which builds leaving's days, may add it. The contract's example for the code carries `target` (`packages/contracts/src/fixtures/examples.ts`); this is a reading of the contract, recorded in `docs/QUESTIONS.md` under FQ63 ("a reservation in a held day is MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED with no `target`"), and a route test pins the bare answer.
   - It comes after every denying fact (anyone the day would deny gets the neutral NOT_FOUND, held or not; T14) and before the cutoff, so a held day stays MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED after its reveal: it never opens.
   - It tells the caller nothing they can't already see: the caller is a member, and Home and the club list already leave the held day out (`packages/domain/src/authorization/days.ts`). The roster shows a star who left as gone; a star who asked to delete their account stays listed by name until the membership ends (FQ117), but the day is already gone from Home and the club list (round 1's P5).
6. **A paused club's days take reservations** like any other until they open (FQ50's default: "Until the day opens at 7 AM, + Add offers it").
7. **A refusal carries the caller's own facts only** (T4, T15): `target`, the day asked for, as the contract's `Occurrence` at the clock's sample; `allowance`, the caller's own counts and limits in that day; and `next_target`, the same person's next day, "for the person to confirm". The next day is the earliest day of the same star that the club list would offer (`days.ts`' `placeable` rule: scheduled, not held, a star to name) whose reveal is after both the clock's sample and the target's reveal, so a limit refusal never offers the full day again and a missed cutoff never offers a day that has also opened. With none, there is no `next_target`. Nothing is moved (T4).
8. **Idempotency (T19).** `createReservation`'s receipt is the caller's, for the operation and the `Idempotency-Key`, over the path's `occurrence_id` and the body (`packages/domain/src/idempotency/receipt.ts`). The same request under the same key gets its first 201 back byte for byte, even after the cutoff, midnight, or a release of the slot; another request under it is IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT. A key whose receipt an earlier interval of the account made names nothing to the new one (FQ112's default, `reserveAsMember`): NOT_FOUND. The reservation's own `request_key` is the same key, and `UNIQUE (interval_id, request_key)` is the database's last guard. `releaseReservation` takes no key (the contract): repeating it returns the same state.

## 1. What each route answers

### `createReservation`, one command (TECH-DESIGN section 5)

1. **Who may add to the day, with no lock** (`loadAddTarget` and `addTargetOutcome`, in the authorization module): the day exists, it is `scheduled`, its club isn't suspended, the caller's account is active with an active interval in that club, and the day hadn't closed when that interval began (a day that closed before the caller joined is NOT_FOUND, as `getOccurrence` says for Q06; round 1's P1). Otherwise the neutral NOT_FOUND, before the receipt is looked at (T14; FQ112's default), for a first request and a retry alike.
2. **The receipt** (`reserveAsMember`, with the clock's first sample as its `created_at`): a replay returns its stored answer here.
3. **The locks, in TECH-DESIGN's order:** the club's row `FOR SHARE`, the occurrence's `FOR SHARE`, the caller's and the star's membership rows `FOR SHARE` (by interval id, byte by byte), then the counter: `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, then `SELECT … FOR NO KEY UPDATE`. That lock conflicts with itself and with `FOR SHARE` as `FOR UPDATE` does, but not with the `FOR KEY SHARE` a foreign key check takes, so a B3 commit that inserts a row naming the counter and then waits for its lock can't deadlock with a command holding it; the counter's key never changes (`contribution_counters_keep`; round 1's S3). A `FOR SHARE` holds off a suspension, a cancellation, a leave or the end of an interval until the command commits, while two contributors' reservations wait only for each other's counter, never for each other.
4. **The clock, sampled after the locks** (T1, T3), and the facts read again under them.
5. **The policy, in this order:**
   - the step 1 conditions again (a leave or a suspension may have come in between, and a rejoined account has another interval): NOT_FOUND;
   - a held day (section 0.5): MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED;
   - the cutoff, `at >= reveal_at` (T3): OCCURRENCE_CLOSED with `target` (its phase `open` or `past` at `at`) and `next_target`;
   - the lapsed reservations settled (section 0.3), then the caller's limits by role, counting committed drops and live reservations (T17): QUOTA_FULL when the whole allowance is used; then, for a video link, EXTERNAL_LIMIT when the role's links are used; for a photo, PHOTO_LIMIT when a friend's two photos are used (never the star's, whose photos are the whole allowance). Each with `target` (`collecting`), `allowance` and `next_target`.
6. **The write:** the reservation, `live`, `created_at` the sample, `expires_at` by section 0.4, `request_key` the key; the counter's `reserved_total`, and `reserved_external` for a link, one more.
7. **The answer:** 201 with the `Reservation` (`id`, `occurrence_id`, `media_class`, `expires_at` in whole seconds rounded down, never later than the database's, and the caller's `allowance` after the write), stored in the receipt and answered as the receipt keeps it.

### `releaseReservation`

1. **Whose it is, with no lock** (`ownsReservation`, in the authorization module beside `addTargetOutcome`; round 1's P3): the reservation exists, its interval is the caller's active interval, and the day is one the caller may add to or could have (not `not_found` by step 1 of `createReservation`). Otherwise NOT_FOUND (T14): another person's reservation, an earlier interval's after a rejoin, a former member's, a guessed id. A reservation in a day since cancelled or suspended, or in a club since suspended, is NOT_FOUND to its holder too: the day is nothing the caller may act on (T9, T14), and its live slot lapses at its own time and costs nothing. "Repeating it returns the same state" holds while the day is one the caller can act on (round 1's R4).
2. **The locks:** club, occurrence and the caller's membership `FOR SHARE`, the counter `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, then the reservation `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (as the counter's, step 3 of `createReservation`). Every change of a reservation's state happens under its counter's lock, so its state read after that lock is final for the command.
3. **The clock after the locks**, and step 1 again.
4. **The lapsed reservations settled**, this one among them if its time has come. Then a reservation still live is `released`, with `ended_at` the sample, and taken off the counts.
5. **The answer:** 200 with `ReleasedReservation`: its `id`, its `state` (`released`, `expired` or `consumed`), the `drop_id` of the caller's own drop that used it when consumed (T20), and the caller's `allowance` in that day. Repeating it returns the same state. A held day's reservation can be released like any other: the slot is the caller's own.

## 2. Code

- `packages/domain/src/authorization/add-target.ts`: `loadAddTarget(tx, userId, occurrenceId)`, the facts of one day for one caller (the day's `DayFacts` as `days.ts` reads them, the caller's account, interval and its `joined_at`, the club's suspension, and the star's later days); `ownsReservation(facts, slotInterval, now)`, whose a reservation is; and `addTargetOutcome(facts, now)`, the pure rule of section 1's steps 1 and 5's first three, returning `not_found`, `held`, `closed` with `target` and `next`, or `collecting` with the caller's interval, `isStar`, `target` and `next`. It reuses `days.ts`' own rules (`placeable`, `chosen`, `chooseNextDayOf`), exported for it, so who may add and what Home offers can't drift.
- `packages/domain/src/drops/allowance.ts`: the two roles' limits, `AllowanceCounts`, `allowanceOf(counter, isStar)` and `limitRefusal(counts, mediaClass)`, pure.
- `packages/domain/src/drops/counter.ts`: `lockCounter(tx, key)` (the row made if missing, and locked), `settleLapsed(tx, key, at)` (its lapsed reservations settled, the row returned), `holdSlot` and `freeSlot`.
- `packages/domain/src/drops/reserve.ts`: `createReservation(tx, clock, input, render)`; `packages/domain/src/drops/release.ts`: `releaseReservation(tx, clock, input)`.
- `packages/domain/src/refused.ts`: `RefusalFacts` gains `target`, `next_target` and `allowance`, as domain values the route renders.
- `services/api/src/drops/reservations.ts`: the two handlers, parsing by the contract's schemas, and `reservationBody` and `releasedBody`, explicit field lists (T55). `services/api/src/bodies.ts`: `occurrenceBody`, moved from `clubs/list.ts`, and `allowanceBody`, so `answers.ts`' `refusal` can render a refusal's days and allowance without an import cycle. `app.ts`: the handlers and their body limits.
- `tests/src/matrix.ts`: `withdraw` and `withdrawUnrevealed` give back the slot of a drop withdrawn before its reveal, as spec 11 says and B4's withdrawal will, so the world's counters agree with its drops.

## 5. Deliberate breakages

One-line changes in a scratch worktree, each of which some test must fail: the clock sampled before the locks; the counter read without its row lock; the role read from the account instead of the interval; lapsed reservations counted; lapse at `<` instead of `<=`; the cutoff at `>` instead of `>=`; QUOTA_FULL after the others; a friend's photo limit at three; the star's link limit at three; `expires_at` not capped; a release that frees a link as a photo; a release of another member's reservation; the receipt looked at before the membership; `next_target` the target itself; the allowance summed across the account's intervals; and the judge's own rules, one at a time. Round 1 adds: a lapsed slot ended at the sample rather than its `expires_at`; the clock sampled between the day's lock and the counter's; the held day checked before the denying facts; the Q06 check dropped, in the command and in the judge; the judge's settled slots taken from any day; and the matrix's slot of Cleo's given back to anyone. Their first run left the first of these uncaught, which showed that `toMatchObject` takes any two `Temporal.Instant` values as equal: the database tests now match a reservation's instants as strings. Round 2 adds its reviewer's three: the caller's `joined_at` read from their first interval rather than the active one; a settle that takes the interval's lapsed slots from every day; the counter locked `FOR SHARE`. The Postgres tests catch all three.

## 8. What later slices owe

- **B3:** `createUpload`'s `drop_photo` purpose names a live reservation of the caller's (`DropPhotoUploadRequest`); `createDrop` consumes the reservation it names, or takes a free slot when it has lapsed, through `lockCounter`, `settleLapsed` and `limitRefusal`, under the same lock order; a purge of lapsed uploads settles reservations only through `settleLapsed`, under `lockCounter`. A read of the caller's own allowance that takes no lock (`getOwnDay`'s `OwnDay.allowance`, B3 and B4) counts live reservations with `expires_at` after its clock sample, never the counter's reserved counts as they stand (section 0.3).
- **The account deletion command** (its own slice): a deletion request holds the star's days from that instant (FQ63's reading for T27) but changes no membership row, so a reservation's `FOR SHARE` on the star's membership doesn't hold it off. The command locks the account's membership rows `FOR UPDATE` before it writes the request, so a reservation in flight finishes first and the next one finds the day held (round 1's R5).
- **B4:** withdrawal before the reveal gives back its slot (the counter's `committed_total`), as the matrix world's helpers now do; a replacement that changes a drop's kind is checked again by `limitRefusal` (T17).
- **B6:** a held day's stage, and whether MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED then carries it as `target` (section 0.5).
- **Gate A's report:** Q01, Q02 and Q04 are proved by B2's schedule PRs' tests, but their audit-map rows still say planned; the report fills them from those PRs' tests.
