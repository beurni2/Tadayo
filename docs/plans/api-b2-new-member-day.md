# api/b2-new-member-day: a new member picks a free day

B2 · api · the schedule after a club's first days, as the founder answered FQ125 to FQ127 on 2026-11-20 at 12:06 UTC, in the api thread:

> FQ125 : no reshuffling everyone keeps their day, FQ126 : no need to confirm again everyone keeps what they already had. FQ127: a new approved member just takes an available day and everything keeps going. A new approved doesn't need to wait four to five weeks for a first, once the new member picks his available day the next week of that on day is his day.

PR #84 (`d02fb0c`) built the defaults the founder has now replaced: a week every active member confirms as a whole, spec 05's whole week again between three and four members, a new change and every confirmation again at each join or leave, and a start four to five weeks out. This slice replaces that flow. Nothing of PR #84 has reached a club: no club exists on `dev`, and the worker there is stopped.

## 0. One PR, the contract with it

**`api/b2-new-member-day`: the contract's words and examples, and the build.** The lanes' rule is that the api lane changes the contract in its own PR (`docs/MILESTONES.md`, "Lanes"). Here the two can't be split without a red PR: the server's serializer tests compare its answers with the contract's own examples (`services/api/src/schedule/routes.test.ts`), so new examples fail the old server and the new server fails the old examples. The Swift client calls no schedule operation yet (`TadayoAPIClient.swift`), so the shapes change freely (the api and ios agreement of 2026-11-05), and the ios lane regenerates `TadayoAPI` after the merge as after any contract change. The PR says so, for the founder to accept or have it split.

It holds: the rules, the rows, the read, the choice and the confirmation; migration `0015`; the contract (`ScheduleProposal`'s membership change, `Schedule`, the slots, the choice and the confirmation, the errors, and the examples); the tests and the privacy matrix. `api/b2-change-start` (FQ128) is dropped: a new member's day no longer waits for a change to start.

## 1. The rules (founder's answers, and the readings that fill them in)

- **Who is asked: only the new members (FQ126).** Once a club's first days have applied, a join opens nothing for the members already in the week. The members who stay keep exactly the days they had (FQ125), and nobody confirms anything again.
- **A new member picks from the free days (FQ127).** Once approved (the roster applied), a member with no day in the club's latest week gets a proposal of their own: every day of the week in force is shown taken by its member (book p11), with no first date (that date is when the day first opened, long past; only the caller's own confirmed day carries one), and every free day is available. They choose and confirm as in the first days (`chooseScheduleDays`, then `confirmScheduleDays`; FQ48, FQ55): two new members at once can hold the same free day, which is contested until one chooses another, and each confirmed day goes ahead at once.
- **A free day** is a weekday no active member has in the club's latest week: one nobody ever had, a member's who left, or one a fourth member's join freed.
- **Only when too few days are free (the review's reading of the founder's answers, 2026-11-20).** Days are freed only where the free days can't give each member with no day spec 05's number for the club's size: three who hold all six days, and a fourth. Where enough are free, nobody's day is freed and nothing is cancelled (FQ125: "no reshuffling everyone keeps their day"; FQ127: "just takes an available day"). So two members and a third who hasn't confirmed, joined by a fourth, keep both their days, and the third and the fourth pick one each from the two free days; so do two joined by two at once. Not cancelling is the reversible reading: a cancelled day never opens again (FQ131).
- **How many (FQ131, new; Claude Code's reading).** Spec 05's number for the club's size after the join: two in a club of two or three, one in a club of four to six. From two to three members the new member gets the two days the others don't have. From three to four the three hold all six days. The founder chose "Free a day now" on the card in the api thread at 12:40 UTC on 2026-11-20: "The three keep their first day; their announced second days from next week are cancelled (anything added for them stays hidden), and the new member starts next week." So the fourth member's join writes a new week at once holding each of the three's first day from Monday, and cancels their days on the freed weekdays from the next Monday on (`status = 'cancelled'`, T9); their days this week stay as announced. The same happens wherever the free days are too few: two joined by three, or the first days applying with a late joiner (FQ100) and all six days held, which frees the second day the last member just confirmed too. The fourth picks from the three freed days, and their day first opens in the week after the confirmation, as any new member's (FQ130).
- **After a leave, nothing is asked (FQ125, FQ126).** The leaver's days are free; every member who stays keeps their days, even where spec 05's table would give a club of three two days each (FQ131). A club under two members is paused (FQ16). A new member who leaves before confirming drops out of their proposal; it ends when nobody it includes is left.
- **When the day first opens (FQ130, new).** "The next week of that day": the picked weekday in the week after the confirmation (Monday to Saturday after the coming Sunday), on the first such date whose 7 AM is at least 24 elapsed hours after the confirmation (FQ79's lead time), and on which no other day of the club is announced. So a new member waits a week or two, not four to five.
- **A leaver's held days on the picked weekday (FQ129, new).** A member who left keeps their announced days held: they never open, and what was added for them stays hidden with its authors (FQ63). Those dates would keep the new member waiting up to five weeks. So from the new member's first date on, the leaver's held days on that weekday are cancelled (`status = 'cancelled'`, which only denies a day, T9), and the new member's days are made on those dates. Nothing is deleted, and the drops on a cancelled day stay with their authors, hidden, as FQ63 keeps them.
- **The announced days never move to someone else (T6, spec 05).** No day goes to another member: a day a fourth member's join frees, or a leaver's day on a picked weekday, is cancelled, and a new day is made on its date only for the new member who picked it, from their first date. The founder's three-to-four answer is the one place a member still in the club loses announced days (from next week on, with what was added for them kept hidden with its authors).

## 2. The writes

- **`settleChange`** (`packages/domain/src/schedule/settle-change.ts`, rewritten), in the caller's transaction under the club's row with its clock sample (T1), where PR #84 calls it (`applyIfReady`, `leaveClub`, the confirmation that applies the first days): in a club whose first days have applied and that has two members or more, the club's members with no day in its latest week get a proposal (`reason = 'membership_change'`), one per club, which includes exactly them: opened when there is none, given a row for a member who joins while it is open, and ended (`superseded`, next `version`) when nobody it includes is still a member or the club is paused. Each member it includes holds spec 05's number of free days at first (the table's day for their place when free, else the first free from Monday, FQ125's earlier rule for a member with no day), until they choose (FQ48).
- **The confirmation** (`confirm-days.ts`): exactly the days the caller holds, none contested (FQ55, FQ99). It writes a new week (`schedule_versions`) at once, from the club's local date of the confirmation, or the day after the latest week's start when that is today or later, so no week is ever rewritten (`change.ts` `nextWeekStart`). The week holds every active member's days of the latest week as they were, with their own first dates, and the caller's new days with theirs (FQ130), and the confirmation cancels the held days of members who left on those weekdays from that date (FQ129). The proposal applies when everyone it includes has confirmed.
- **A fourth member's join** (`settleChange`, `freed`), where too few days are free: a new week from the same start, holding each member's first day from Monday as it was; their days on the freed weekdays from the next Monday on cancelled.
- **Generation** (`generate.ts`, `make-days.ts`): PR #84's hold at an open change's start goes; days are made from the week in force on each date, as before PR #84, and never on a date cancelled for that star.
- **Migration `0015_new_member_day.sql`:** `occurrences`' one-day-per-date rule counts only days not cancelled (the partial unique index `occurrences_one_live_day` in place of `UNIQUE (club_id, local_date)`); `schedule_assignment_keeps_announced`, at commit, refuses a week day that would give a date with a live day to someone else (FQ61 said of the days, since a week may now start before days already made); `schedule_assignment_follows_week`, at commit, refuses a week day after the first days that is neither its member's day in the week before, as it was, nor their confirmation of a pick in the week that confirmation wrote; `schedule_version_keeps_members`, at commit, refuses a week after the first days that leaves out a day of the week before whose member is still in the club, but for the freed days where too few are free; `schedule_pick_member_guard` lets a pick include only members still in the club with no day in its latest week; `day_not_cancelled_guard` gives a cancelled day no new reservation or drop; 0005's `schedule_versions_insert_guard` (a week now starts after every earlier week of its club), 0014's `schedule_proposals_guard`, `schedule_change_start_guard` and `schedule_proposal_days_guard`, and 0013's `schedule_confirmation_guard` (a member who left contests nothing in a pick) replaced so a pick's members choose and confirm as first days' do, never hold a day of the latest week whose member is still in the club, and its confirmation writes a week. Two plain indexes, on a club's days and on its proposals. No table, column or row is dropped or rewritten; one constraint becomes an index of the same rule for the days not cancelled.

## 3. The read

- **`getSchedule`**: for a member the proposal includes, `proposal` with `reason: membership_change`, `slots` (theirs, with `first_date` once confirmed; others' taken with `owner` and no date; free ones available, `held_by` or `holders` as in the first days), `my_days`, `my_state`; `current`. For every other member, `current` alone: nobody else is asked anything. `announced` goes from the contract: nobody it was for is asked to confirm a week now.
- **`current` holds only members still in the club** (the review, 2026-11-20): a leaver's days won't open (FQ63) and are free in a pick, and a member who joined since never shared the club with them, so their name stays out of the week too.
- **The contract:** `ScheduleProposal`'s membership change, `Schedule`, `ScheduleChoiceRequest`, `ScheduleConfirmRequest`, `confirmScheduleDays` and `chooseScheduleDays` described as above; the examples `getSchedule.change` and `change_confirmed` replaced by `getSchedule.new_member`, and `confirmScheduleDays.confirmed` made again, for Kitchen table's new member Leila picking Friday, the one free day; the descriptions PR #84 listed as false corrected with them.

## 6. Risks and open points

- **Three to four**, answered by the founder on the card ("Free a day now", 12:40 UTC): built as answered. It is the one rule that cancels announced days of members still in the club.
- **FQ129 and the three-to-four answer cancel days.** A leaver's held days on a picked weekday would never have opened; a freed day's were announced. Nothing is deleted, and drops on a cancelled day stay hidden with their authors (T9), but these are the first commands that cancel a day.
- **Design.** G04-change draws a week everyone confirms. The new member's pick is the first days' day picker with the others' days taken, and the members already in the week see nothing new but the new member's day in the club's week, from the confirmation on (it first opens the week after), as a member's confirmed first day shows in the first days (FQ78; T33: nothing waits on anyone, and nothing is held back to hide who has picked); the ios lane and Claude Design are told through the coordinator.
- **Consequences of the founder's three-to-four answer, followed as given** (FQ131): a fourth member who leaves before picking leaves the three's second days cancelled, and nobody takes a second day again after a leave; and the first days applying with a late joiner and all six days held free the last member's just-confirmed second day. A new member joining two takes two days, spec 05's number for three.
