This file is not updated after PR 1. The measurements are one reader's, taken from the sheets' pixels.

Read from the `ios` run's 28 contact sheets (run 42960811472: each new frame beside today's snapshot, on the `evidence` branch under `ios/b0-adopt-records/1ee1330/ios/sheets/`), with the ink rows of both panels measured in points. Where "the build is 13 points lower" is said, about 5 of them are that title and 8 are the gap under the bar.
