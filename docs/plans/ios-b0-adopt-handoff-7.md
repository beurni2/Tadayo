# Adopting handoff 7 for the screens on `main`: plan (ios lane, B0)

Written on 11 November 2026 by the ios thread, from `main` at `ea149b3`, after five readers mapped the slice (sign-in, the profile and the Avatar, the root and the tabs, the tests and the split, the records) and a sixth criticised their maps for gaps. Their six reports are committed beside this plan as appendices (`docs/plans/ios-b0-adopt-handoff-7/`: `signin.md`, `profile.md`, `root.md`, `tests.md`, `records.md`, `gaps.md`) and hold the detail: every measure, string key, test and reference. A seventh file beside them, `baseline.md`, is the ios thread's own reading of the 28 baseline sheets: today's build against each frame, state by state, with the PR that closes each difference. **Where an appendix and this plan disagree, this plan wins**; section 3 lists the corrections. The plan is pasted, PR by PR, into each PR's Plan section.

Sources: the design thread's check of handoff 7's export (`docs/design/handoff-7/check/handoff-7-check.md`; its section 8's first list is this slice's work list) and its findings (`docs/design/handoff-7/check/handoff-7-findings.md`, cited by id: F, W, R, X, V, D, C, BM, FM, RM, VM, WM); the delivery (`design/reference/claude-design/handoff-7/`: `IMPLEMENTATION.md`, `component-contracts.json`, `copy-contracts.json`, `screen-copy.json`, the 29 frames and the component sheet); the check's X-33 (INVENTORY rows) and X-34 (QUESTIONS). Spec 23, 24, 29; book p26, p27, p34, p35; T41 to T43, T45 to T47, T49.

## 1. Scope

What handoff 7 changes on `main`: the screens `ios/b0-sign-in` (#25) and `ios/b0-tabs` (#26) built.

- **Sign-in and the profile:** A02-email, A02-code, A02-age and A03-profile. The busy button (a native indicator beside the label), read-only fields while a request runs, failure lines in their own blocks, "Leave sign-in" as a text action in the navigation bar, text buttons on the left, the profile's arrival focus, its keyboard-aware footer, the name's too-long line, and the Avatar's person symbol when the name gives no initial.
- **The root:** the start-up wait ("Loading your account…"), "Leaving sign-in…", the three failures' geometry and hints.
- **The header before a club and the three tabs with no club:** the header's rectangles and stacking at the accessibility sizes, the opaque band behind the status bar, the page titles 32 under the header, a Retry hint per tab, Private drafts' folder 16 in.

Already done, so not here: the drop card group of the check's section 8 (`ios/b0-drop-card`, #28) and the day header, person row and target block (`ios/b0-lab-rows`). Not ours: FQ69's owed retention proposal (how long an account that stopped at the age or the profile is kept) is the api lane's, since retention is on the server; this slice makes Leave sign-in a visible button and says so in PR 3b, and invents no period. The pack twice mentions a "guest-contribution/SMS proposal" the brief never made (W-16, R-14, the check's line on it); nothing is built or asked for it.

## 2. What wins when sources disagree

The check's own order, kept: (1) the founder's answers and Defaults (FQ58, FQ85, FQ89, FQ90, FQ68, FQ86, FQ87); (2) the critic's rulings and the check's section 8, "Kept as built" (the body lines stay `secondaryText`, the failure title stays `emptyTitle` 25, the no-club gaps and G06's two lines as one text stay); (3) the delivery's written contracts (`component-contracts.json`, `copy-contracts.json`, `IMPLEMENTATION.md`); (4) its frames; (5) each finding's own "Meanwhile". `replaces` scopes limit what handoff 7 wins (`precedence.brand`). ## 4. Decisions

### 4.1 For the founder, with defaults that stand until answered

- **FQ93, whether Leave sign-in keeps the typed address.** Default: an empty field, as built. Handoff 7 keeps the address in memory (`ageExit.return`); the critic ruled that a data-handling choice, not a design correction.
- **FQ96, whether the start-up wait shows when there is nothing to load.** Default: the wait shows, and is spoken, only once a sign-in is found to read an account for; with none, the canvas goes straight to A02-email, as today. "Leaving sign-in…" shows whenever a session is being removed. The check's reading (always, at once) is one line in `RootModel.start()` away.
- Handoff 7 agrees by name with FQ89 and FQ90 and with the Defaults of FQ58, FQ69, FQ70 and FQ86; those bullets are in PR 1. If the founder answers any of them, the answer is recorded as ADR-033 (the number held for this slice) in the PR that builds on it.

## 11. Risks

- **macOS minutes** (section 8): batch pushes.
- **Binary growth:** about 200 reference writes and 12 MB of frames and the sheet; the repository never forgets a blob.
- **Spinner scale:** a 16-point indicator is a `UIActivityIndicatorView` scaled or constrained; `Spinners.check` needs one in the tree while `busy`, and the stilling swizzle is on `startAnimating` (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`: the indicator's frame otherwise differs run to run). If the scaled indicator draws differently from the frame, record a Known difference; don't replace the indicator (T45).
- **Flaky hosted VoiceOver:** the reader depends on `_AXSSetAutomationEnabled` and a 10-second probe (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`); more readings mean more exposure. They already ran green on `main`.
- **`tools-tests` reads `INVENTORY.md`** (`tools/ci/ios_steps_test.py`, at least 149 states): keep every row's shape; renaming a state drops it from the set.
- **The old frames' differences recorded in INVENTORY go stale** if a PR forgets to rewrite them: each PR owns the rows and Known differences for what it builds.
- **A state named differently by two PRs** silently loses its render beside its snapshot: D6's names are the contract.
- **UI tests are slow** (about 21 seconds each); three more cost about a minute a run.
- **`ios/b0-lab-rows` (#31) merged first** (`339fac2`; its records were rebuilt into this branch's merge of `main`). It also changed the package's `Localizable.xcstrings` (+204 lines), `Metrics.swift` and `TypeRole.swift`, the Lab (`LabView.swift`, `LabSnapshotTests.swift`, `FixtureCast.swift`), and added `TadayoTests/Reading.swift` (`Read`, `assertReads`: the hosted readings' helper). The PRs that follow (3a to 6) edit `Metrics.swift` and the Lab's sections on top of it (3a adds one key to the package's catalog; the others add to the app's, which #31 left alone), and a hosted reading this slice adds to `TadayoTests` (TH-03) uses `Reading.swift`, which only that target compiles, rather than a second helper.
