# B2: Spike S1's server half, its second PR: the commit (`api/b2-spike-commit`)

`POST /v1/occurrences/{occurrence_id}/drops`, the contract's `createDrop`, for a photo (spec 06, 07, 11, 14, 32; TECH-DESIGN sections 5, 6 and 8; ADR-024; T1, T3, T4, T8, T9, T12, T14, T15, T17, T18, T19, T20, T21, T52, T55). Made from `main` at `2ff93ca`. It is block 1.2's step (w).

## 0. Where this sits, and the readings

1. **The second of spike S1's two server PRs.** `docs/plans/api-b2-spike-uploads.md` section 0.1: "the commit, `api/b2-spike-commit` (`createDrop` for a photo: the receipt, the locks, the clock after them, the role's limits with the reservation consumed, the immutable audience and its seal; T3, T8, T17, T19, T20)". With it, the share extension's add is `createReservation`, `createUpload`, the `PUT`, `completeUpload`, `getUpload` until `ready`, then `createDrop`, and only its 201 means Added (T20). The device run of S1 needs it on `dev` with the `dev` worker started (an `ops` run on the founder's yes).
2. **A photo only.** `CreateDropRequest.media` is a photo upload or an external video's source. A source comes only from `resolveSource` (`POST /v1/sources/resolve`), which isn't built: no account can hold one, so a link's commit has nothing to commit. A body whose media is `external_video` is answered as a source the caller doesn't have, the neutral NOT_FOUND, as the unbuilt routes answer (T14), after the same first step as a photo's. The link's commit (its source owned, unused and live; its scoped identity; `CAPTION_CONTAINS_SOURCE`; EXTERNAL_LIMIT) comes with `resolveSource`, in the slice that builds the composer's link (B4), and section 8 says what it owes.
3. **A photo's slot is its upload's reservation.** A drop's photo upload is made only under one of the caller's live photo reservations, one photo to a slot (migration 0016), so the upload names its slot. The commit uses that slot: `reservation_id`, when the body sends it, must name the same reservation; any other id is answered as one the caller doesn't have, NOT_FOUND, since a commit that used another slot would leave the upload's slot held by a photo already added. Without `reservation_id`, the upload's own reservation is the slot. This is the reading of `CreateDropRequest`'s "`reservation_id` uses a held slot; without one, a free slot is taken at commit" for a photo, whose upload always has one; for a link, which may have none, the sentence applies as it stands.
4. **A slot that has ended counts as none.** The upload's reservation, once the counter's lapsed ones are settled under its lock (`settleLapsed`), is `live` or not. Live: the commit consumes it (`consumed`, `ended_at` the sample, `drop_id` the new drop), and the counter moves one slot from reserved to committed, so no limit can refuse it. Ended (`expired`, or `released` when the app kept the photo as a draft and adds it later): a free slot is taken if the caller's limits by role allow one, else the refusal the contract names, QUOTA_FULL or PHOTO_LIMIT, QUOTA_FULL first, with the day, the caller's own allowance and the same person's next day (T4, T15, T17; `limitRefusal`, as `createReservation` uses it). An ended reservation never changes again (`app.reservations_guard`), so `releaseReservation` of it still answers `expired` or `released`, not `consumed`: the commit didn't use it. An app that lost the commit's answer replays `createDrop` under the same key, which is what says Added (T19, T20); if it starts a new add of the same photo instead, the commit answers DUPLICATE_DROP with the drop it has (section 0.6).
5. **Only a ready upload is committed** (TECH-DESIGN section 5, step 3). `pending`, `preparing`, `held` and `rejected` are MEDIA_NOT_READY: a held upload, on a moderation hold, isn't ready, and a rejected one never becomes so (the contract's `completeUpload`). `ready` is final (`app.uploads_state_guard`), so an upload read ready stays ready.
6. **One drop of a photo to a day for each contributor** (T21; spec 11). The drop's `dedupe_key` is its upload's scoped content hash (HMAC-SHA-256 of the original's bytes keyed by the owner, `docs/plans/api-b2-spike-prepare.md` section 0.7), so the same photo uploaded twice by the same account matches, and nobody else's does. A commit whose hash matches one of the caller's own active or held drops in the day is DUPLICATE_DROP with `existing_drop_id`, that drop's id, to offer View existing; nothing else of it. The counter's lock puts two commits of the same contributor in one day in order, so the second sees the first's drop; `drops_no_duplicate` is the last guard. An upload already used by a drop that has since been withdrawn or removed can't become another (`drops.upload_id` is unique): NOT_FOUND, as an upload that is no longer a pending item.
7. **The upload must be the caller's, live, for this day.** `ownsUpload` (the caller's account's own, before its `expires_at`), `purpose` `drop_photo`, and its reservation made by the caller's active interval for the path's day. Anything else, an id nobody has included, is NOT_FOUND (T14): an upload made under a slot of another day is never committed to this one (T4: nothing is moved), and a lapsed upload is nobody's (`docs/plans/api-b2-spike-uploads.md` section 0.6: "the commit will too"). After a rejoin, an upload made under the earlier interval's slot is the account's still but the slot is not the new interval's, so it is NOT_FOUND too.
8. **The audience is every active member of the club at the commit, the author and the star among them, written once and sealed** (T8; spec 07). The club's row is held `FOR SHARE` for the whole command (`lockDay`), and accepting an invitation, leaving and applying a roster take it `FOR UPDATE`, so no membership begins or ends between the audience's rows and the seal; the deferred `app.drops_sealed` check holds the audience to the club's active members at COMMIT, and `app.drop_audience_guard` refuses any row after the seal.
9. **The caption.** Optional (spec 10). The contract's `Caption` checks it at the route (at most 140 grapheme clusters, well formed). An empty one is none: the drop's `caption` and its first `drop_caption_versions` row are null. Otherwise it is stored as sent. A photo has no source URL, so CAPTION_CONTAINS_SOURCE can't apply. The caption never reaches the receipt's stored answer, a log line or an outbox event (T52; TECH-DESIGN section 6); the receipt keeps only the request's hash, so another caption under the same key is IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT.
10. **Idempotency (T19, Q12).** The receipt is the caller's, for `createDrop` and the key, over the path's `occurrence_id` and the body as the contract parsed it. The same request under the same key gets its first 201 back, as it was, after the cutoff, midnight or a release; another request under it is IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT; a key an earlier interval of the account used is NOT_FOUND to the new one (`reserveAsMember`, FQ112's default). Spec 06: "Idempotent retry after cutoff of an already committed drop: Return original committed result without another drop". The one part of the first answer a retry doesn't repeat as it was is the day's star, a `Person`, whom every answer names as members see them as it is sent (FQ75): the receipt keeps the star by interval alone, and the first answer and each retry name them then, written where the first answer had them; a retry once the star has no name left to show (an ended interval that kept none) is MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED, as a new commit to that day is (FQ139, raised by this PR, its default as built; found by the privacy matrix, whose star role renames itself in each phase). So no copy of a person's name outlives their deletion in another account's receipt (T27).
11. **No outbox event yet.** TECH-DESIGN section 5's step 5 appends a command's events. Nothing consumes a committed drop's event yet: the reveal's manifest, the notification policy and analytics' `drop committed` each come with their consumer (B3, B5, B9), which writes its producer then. A stopped worker changes no answer (T1, Q18).
12. **The answer** is the contract's `DropReceipt`: `drop_id`, `occurrence` (the day as the contract's `Occurrence` at the clock's sample, `collecting`), `media_class` `photo`, `state` `added`, `version` 1, `allowance` (the caller's own, after the write, T15) and `highlight` (the caller's own pointer in the day as it stands: `{ version: 0 }` before the first, T22). No caption, no upload id, no URL (TECH-DESIGN section 6). The day's star in it is named as members see them as each answer is sent (section 0.10, FQ139).

## 1. `createDrop`, one command (TECH-DESIGN section 5)

1. **The request**, parsed by the contract's `CreateDropRequest`: the `Idempotency-Key` first, then the body, strict (an audience, a role, a time or a target field is INVALID_REQUEST). The gate answers PROFILE_INCOMPLETE to an account that hasn't finished its profile (`createDrop` isn't `beforeProfile`).
2. **Who may add to the day, with no lock** (`loadAddTarget`, `addTargetOutcome`): anything but a member of the club whose day it is, scheduled, in a club that isn't suspended, a day still open when the caller's interval began, is NOT_FOUND, before the receipt (T14; Q06).
3. **The receipt** (`reserveAsMember`, the clock's first sample its `created_at`): a replay returns its stored answer here.
4. **The locks, in TECH-DESIGN's order:** the club's row and the day's `FOR SHARE`, the caller's and the star's membership rows `FOR SHARE` (`lockDay`), the counter `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (`lockCounter`), then the upload's row `FOR SHARE` and its reservation's `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`. A release takes the counter's lock and then the reservation's (`releaseReservation`), and preparation changes an upload's row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` without either (`packages/domain/src/media/prepare.ts` `recordPreparation`), so no two commands wait on each other in opposite orders, and a commit that meets a preparation in flight waits for it and reads the upload as it left it. The drop's insert then takes `FOR KEY SHARE` on the upload, the counter and the day, which none of these conflicts with.
5. **The clock, sampled after the locks** (T1, T3): a commit that waited across 07:00 reads the time after it.
6. **The policy, in this order,** on the facts read under the locks:
   - step 2's rule again: NOT_FOUND (a leave, a suspension or a rejoin came in between);
   - a held day, whose star has gone: MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED (FQ63);
   - from the day's reveal on: OCCURRENCE_CLOSED with `target` and the same person's `next_target` (T3, T4; spec 06's "Upload started before but completed after cutoff: Preserve as draft; ask whether to submit to next date");
   - a link: NOT_FOUND (section 0.2);
   - the upload: the caller's, live, a drop's photo, under a slot of the caller's interval in this day, and `reservation_id` naming that slot when sent, else NOT_FOUND (sections 0.3, 0.7);
   - not ready: MEDIA_NOT_READY (section 0.5);
   - a duplicate of the caller's own active or held drop in the day: DUPLICATE_DROP with `existing_drop_id`; an upload already used by a drop: NOT_FOUND (section 0.6);
   - the counter's lapsed reservations settled; the upload's slot still live, or, when it has ended, the caller's limits by role: QUOTA_FULL or PHOTO_LIMIT, QUOTA_FULL first, with `target`, `allowance` and `next_target` (section 0.4).
7. **The write:** the drop (`drp_` id, the day, the club, the caller's interval, `photo`, the upload, its hash as `dedupe_key`, the caption or null, `active`, version 1, `submitted_at` and `updated_at` the sample); its caption's first version; the reservation `consumed` when live, else nothing; the counter (`committed_total` one more, and `reserved_total` one less when the slot was consumed); the audience, every active member of the club; the seal, `audience_sealed_at` the sample (section 0.8).
8. **The answer:** 201 with the `DropReceipt` (section 0.12), stored in the receipt with the day's star by interval alone, and each answer, the first and every retry, naming the star as members see them as it is sent (section 0.10, FQ139).

## 6. Review

Full depth (`docs/GITHUB.md`): authorization, the database and idempotency, with a privacy reviewer among the fresh reviewers; every finding fixed or answered in the PR.

Round 1 at `69c3d2c` (two fresh reviewers: the command as built, and privacy). No blocker. Fixed or answered in the second commit:

- **C1, FQ139's default after the reveal.** The default answered a retry MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED when the star's account goes with no name, but the commit's header, FQ139 and the journal's 0.8 spoke only of a day held before the reveal. After it, the drop is active in the opened day and the day can't be named. Answered: the retry is still refused MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED (no answer can be sent without the star), the drop stays active, and B6, which builds account deletion, decides how such a day ends; FQ139, the header (item 2) and 0.8 say so, and `tests/src/db/create-drop.test.ts` "answers a retry MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED too when the star's account goes with no name after the reveal, the drop still active in the opened day (FQ139)" pins it. Unreachable until B6.
- **C2, five audit rows out of date.** T3, T8, T17, Q10 and Q12 still said the commit was B3's. Fixed: each names `createDrop` since this branch and what a video link's commit (B4) still owes; T3 and Q12 are `tested`. ADR-013's own test, a commit held on the day's lock across 07:00, is new: "waits for the day's own row, and reads the clock after it: a commit held on the day across 07:00 is refused (ADR-013)".
- **C3, no concurrent same-key test.** Added: "makes one drop when the same send arrives twice at once: the second waits for the first, then gets its answer" (the first holds its transaction open with the receipt reserved; the second waits on it, then replays the same bytes; one drop, one receipt). A receipt reserved by a look and then an insert, which every other test passes, fails it.
- **C4, the uploads' purge.** A commit never moves an upload's `expires_at`, so a later purge of uploads past it would take a committed drop's photo. Answered in section 8: B3's purge skips every upload a drop names.
- **C5, contract words in a slice PR.** `DropReceipt`'s description (`packages/contracts/src/contributing.ts`, `openapi.yaml`) changes here for FQ139, as PR #87 changed a description in its slice PR: flagged in the PR's body and the journal's 0.8.
- **Privacy P1, the request's hash outlives the caption.** A receipt keeps the SHA-256 of the request, its caption included, after the drop is withdrawn or removed, so someone with a copy of the database could confirm a guessed caption. Answered in section 8: B4, which builds withdrawal and removal, purges those receipts or keys the hash with a server secret (T24). No drop can be withdrawn or removed before B4.
- **Privacy P2, ownership restated in the command.** The commit compared the slot's day itself before asking `ownsReservation`, which compared only the interval. Fixed: `ownsReservation(facts, slot, now)` (`packages/domain/src/authorization/add-target.ts`) takes the slot's day and interval and checks both, for the commit, the photo upload and the release alike, and the commit no longer states it (T9); a unit case and "moves nothing: an upload made for another day, or a reservation_id naming another slot, is not found (T4)" catch its loss. The upload's `owner_user_id` in the commit's query is kept: it scopes the lock, so another account's upload is neither read nor locked, while `ownsUpload` decides.

## 8. What later slices owe

- **The link's commit** (with `resolveSource`, B4): the source the caller's own, unused, before its `expires_at`; its scoped identity as `dedupe_key`; CAPTION_CONTAINS_SOURCE for a caption holding the source's link; a `reservation_id` for a link, consumed when live, else a free slot, with EXTERNAL_LIMIT among the refusals; `sources.used_at` set at the commit. Until then a link is NOT_FOUND (section 0.2).
- **B3:** the reveal's manifest and the media route read committed drops through the read predicate; the commit's outbox event comes with the manifest's job. `getOwnDay` reads the caller's drops and allowance with no lock, counting live reservations by its own clock sample (`docs/plans/api-b2-reservations.md` section 0.3).
- **B3, the uploads' purge:** an upload past its `expires_at` counts as gone only while no drop names it. A commit never moves `expires_at`, so the purge of expired uploads, and of their objects and renditions, skips every upload named by `drops.upload_id` or `drops.pending_upload_id` (round 1 of PR #116's review, C4).
- **B4:** withdrawal gives back the slot and clears the highlight; `updateDrop`'s replacement keeps the slot. Withdrawal and removal also reach the drop's receipts (T24): a receipt's `request_hash` is a plain SHA-256 of the request, its caption included, so B4 purges the receipts of a withdrawn or removed drop's commands, or keys the hash with a server secret, so a guessed caption can't be confirmed from a copy of the database (round 1 of PR #116's review, privacy P1).
