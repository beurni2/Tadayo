## 1. The schedule's shapes (`packages/contracts/src/schemas/clubs.ts`, `operations-clubs.ts`, `errors.ts`)

Every change is to a schema the Swift client can't reach (above), so none needs the three steps, and none adds an enum value. Two kinds of proposal are told apart throughout: the club's **first days** (`reason` `activation`), where each member chooses and each day goes ahead as its member confirms it (FQ55), and a **membership change** (`reason` `membership_change`), one proposed week that every active member confirms as a whole (FQ56; `G04-change`).

### 1.1 Who holds a day

- **In the first days**, a member holds the days proposed for them until they first choose, and from then exactly the days they last chose, none to `days_per_member` (FQ48; handoff 6's `proposedHolder`: "A current proposal holds its day even before the member opens the picker"). A member who has never opened the picker holds their proposed days all the same, and counts in a clash ("The proposed days overlap on {weekday}.", FQ55). Only a confirmed day is taken.
- **In a membership change**, each member holds the days the change proposes for them, and nobody chooses (`changeRows` `"selection": false`).
- **A revision of the first days** (a join or a leave before any day is confirmed, FQ55, FQ56) proposes the week again for the new roster (FQ102, added after the PR's review, narrowed after its second round). Where `days_per_member` stays, every member keeps what they hold, a leaver's days are freed and a new member holds the days it proposes for them, which nobody holds, so nobody is moved (FQ55, T6). Where spec 05's table changes it (between three and four members), every member holds the days it proposes for them, their earlier choices set aside, until they choose again: the one revision that moves members and can end a contest, flagged to the founder in FQ102.
- **A member whose membership ends holds no day** in an open proposal: the days they held are free, and a membership change is replaced by a new proposal (`MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED`, below). What happens to their announced days is FQ63's. Once a day is confirmed, a leave in the first days revises nothing, and moves no `version` unless it leaves only confirmed members among those the first days include: then the first days apply, moving `state` and `version`, as the last confirmation does. The leaver's held days are freed, so a contest they were in may end; a day the leaver confirmed stays `taken` with them as its `owner`, with no `first_date`, its days that haven't opened held (FQ63); `days_per_member` stays (spec 05's table for the roster the first days were last proposed for), and the week for the club's new size comes as a membership change once the first days have applied, as FQ100 does for a join. A club left with one member is paused (FQ16, FQ50): the first days apply first if that member has confirmed, and otherwise the first-days proposal is superseded with no successor and the join that restarts the club opens new first days (FQ102).

### 1.2 What each slot says, and to whom

- **`ScheduleSlotState`**, the same five values, each said for both kinds of proposal:
  - `yours`: the caller holds it and nobody else does, or the caller has confirmed it.
  - `taken`: in the first days, another member has confirmed it; `owner` names them (book p11, FQ78). Never in a membership change, where no day is confirmed on its own.
  - `available`: the caller doesn't hold it, and it isn't `taken`, `clash` or `no_reveal`. In the first days that means nobody has confirmed it and at most one other member holds it; in a membership change, that the change doesn't propose it for the caller.
  - `clash`: in the first days, the caller and one or more other members hold it; none of them can confirm it until all but one have chosen another day, and the one left can then confirm it (FQ55). Never in a membership change.
  - `no_reveal`: in the first days, two or more other members hold it and the caller doesn't: "Contested" and "No reveal scheduled", with no names and no count (FQ46; `G04-contested-other`, `G04-confirmed-others`; `IMPLEMENTATION.md`: "no names or count are serialized"). A caller still choosing may choose it, and then holds it too and sees the names (FQ46, FQ48; handoff 6's X-13). Never in a membership change.
- **`ScheduleSlot.holders`** replaces `clash_with`: on a `clash` slot only, every member who holds the day, **the caller included**, 2 to 6, each once, in `Member.position` order (section 1.4). The app finds the caller by `club.my_membership_id` and draws "Ben · You, Alex and Debby" (`G04-conflict-three`) or "Ben and Debby · You" (`G04-confirm-refused`); listing only the others would leave it no way to place You, since `getSchedule` carries no positions. Only the members who hold the day see any name on it (FQ46).
- **`ScheduleSlot.held_by`** (new, optional), on an `available` slot only, never the caller:
  - in the first days, the one other member who holds the day, given only to a caller still choosing (`my_state` `choosing`), for the day picker (`G04-one-day`, `G04-conflict-three`, `G04-contested-other`, `G04-confirm-refused`: "Maya · Proposed"). A caller who has confirmed sees such a day with no name, as the club's week shows it to everyone (`weekRows.unconfirmed`: "No reveal scheduled, no owner. Same shape as an unused weekday"; handoff 6 has the G04 completion use the same row; FQ48: outside the day-picking screens a day nobody has confirmed names nobody). No frame shows a confirmed caller a day exactly one unconfirmed member holds, so applying that row to `getSchedule` is Claude Code's reading of FQ48 and `weekRows`, flagged in the PR. The app shows `held_by` only on the day picker: the club's week (`G05-schedule`, `G05-schedule-choose-own`) and the G04 completion name nobody on an unconfirmed day, though the same answer serves them;
  - in a membership change, the member the change proposes the day for, given to every caller, whoever has confirmed, the caller or anyone else (`G04-change` names each member's proposed weekdays): neither a member's own confirmation nor anyone else's changes what they see of the week (FQ81).
  - It doesn't say whether the member chose the day or holds it by proposal (handoff 6: "Explanation never claims everyone actively chose"). A change in what a member holds still shows, across two reads, that they chose, as FQ46 and FQ48 accept ("Joining isn't private either"). No frame draws a day one other member holds by choice rather than by proposal (`G04-one-day` draws only "Proposed"); its words are for the ios lane's next handoff, and a field telling the two apart could be added later without breaking anything.
- **`ScheduleSlot.owner`** keeps its meaning: present only on `taken`.
- **`ScheduleSlot.first_date`** becomes optional (section 1.6).

### 1.3 Choosing, confirming, `version` and the refusals

- **`ScheduleChoiceRequest`** (the first days only, while the caller's `my_state` is `choosing`): the weekdays the caller holds from now on, replacing what they held, **none to `days_per_member`**, from slots that are `yours`, `clash` (they may keep it, since nobody is asked to give way, FQ55), `no_reveal` (they join it) or `available` (if another member holds it, it becomes contested); never a `taken` day, never Sunday (T6). In a club of two or three a member may hold one day while they choose the second, and a proposed day they set aside is free (`G04-two-days-one-chosen`: Ben keeps Monday, and Thursday reads "Available · Proposed for you" in his view only; FQ48). A two-day picker's selected row reads "Remove {weekday} from your choices." and tapping it removes that choice, the last one too (`IMPLEMENTATION.md`; each tap's route is "update same proposal selection"), so a choice of none is kept, and the member's proposed days are then free. The one-day picker never sends none ("The single-day own row stays selected"), and the server keeps it all the same. `ChosenWeekdays` takes none, one or two, each once; its description and `ScheduleChoiceRequest`'s change from "Exactly `days_per_member`".
- **`ScheduleConfirmRequest`** gains `weekdays` (`ConfirmedWeekdays`: one or two, each once): the days the caller saw as theirs, so a confirmation never confirms days the caller didn't see (another device changed the choice meanwhile). It confirms them at once, and is refused with `NOT_ALLOWED` unless they are exactly the days the caller holds, `days_per_member` of them (book p11: "the confirmation enables only for two choices"), and none is held by another member too, even one who chose it a moment earlier (FQ55; `G04-confirm-refused`); nothing the caller chose is lost. So in a club of two or three, a member whose one day is contested confirms neither until the clash ends (FQ99). In a membership change, `weekdays` are the days the change proposes for the caller, and the confirmation is of the whole proposed week.
- **What moves `version`** (closes the gap `Schedule` lists, T33): the proposal's `version` moves only with its `state` or a new revision of the proposed week (a member joining or leaving in the club's first days before any day is confirmed: handoff 6's `firstDays.rosterVersion`); never on anyone's choice, and never on a confirmation or a leave that leaves `state` as it was, so `expected_version` never tells a member that someone else has chosen or confirmed. What moves it without a revision is what applies the first days, so moves `state` (below): their last confirmation, or a leave that leaves only confirmed members (FQ102); it tells nobody anything, since every day then has a date. `roster_revisions.version` follows the same rule (`db/migrations/0004_rosters.sql`).
- **When `state` moves:**
  - the first days become `applied` when every member they include who is still active has confirmed (a late joiner, FQ100, is never one of them), on the last confirmation or on a leave that leaves only confirmed members (FQ102), which moves `version` with `state` and which every member sees anyway, since every day is then `taken` or `yours` with a date; that confirmation answers `applied` with the next `version` (`G04-confirmed-many`);
  - a membership change stays `open`, with the same `version`, in `Schedule.proposal`, until `effective_from`, whoever has confirmed: the last member's confirmation answers like any other, and `current` stays the schedule in force today. At `effective_from` it becomes `applied` if every active member confirmed, and otherwise `superseded`, replaced by a new proposal from a later date that everyone confirms again (FQ49; `changeRows.effectiveDatePassed`: "Fresh future version and fresh review, never backdate"). Nothing tells a member before `effective_from` that everyone has confirmed (FQ81; FQ101 for the days it adds). `operations-clubs.ts`' "applies at its boundary" stays, and `ScheduleProposal`'s "It applies once every active member has confirmed" is reworded to match.
- **Which answer refuses what** (closes the gap), in this order:
  - `NOT_FOUND` first, for a caller without an active membership in the proposal's club (T9, T14: knowing an id grants nothing), and for an active member whose current membership the proposal doesn't include, one who left and rejoined among them (FQ100), so `current_id` and `current_version` reach only the members a proposal includes, and a former member listed in a replaced proposal learns nothing of its successor.
  - `MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED` with `current_id`: a newer proposal, with another id, replaced this one: a membership change whose start date passed before everyone confirmed (`G04.error.pastStart`, FQ49), or one replaced because the club's membership changed again. Only a membership change is replaced so: the first days stay open until their days are confirmed (FQ55).
  - `VERSION_CONFLICT`: the proposed week changed and the proposal kept its id, as for a join or a leave in the first days before any day is confirmed (`G04.error.revision`); `current_version` is the new one.
  - `NOT_ALLOWED`: a choice after the caller confirmed (FQ57; `noEditAfterConfirm`), a choice in a membership change, a choice of more than `days_per_member` days, a choice of a day another member has confirmed (`G04.error.chooseTaken`), and a confirmation refused as above. A confirmation can name a day another member has confirmed only when the caller's own choice changed on another device and the day was then taken; that is `G04.error.confirmTaken`. After a `NOT_ALLOWED` the app fetches the schedule again and words the refusal from what it reads: a `taken` day names its owner ("{weekday} is now {ownerName}'s day", FQ78), a `clash` is `G04-confirm-refused`.
  - `chooseScheduleDays`' errors gain `NOT_ALLOWED`, a code that exists (the pinned table in `contract.test.ts` changes on purpose). `ERRORS.NOT_ALLOWED` and `MEMBERSHIP_CHANGED` say the same in their descriptions, with no new code and no new field. The two operations' descriptions say the order.

### 1.4 `Member.position` (FQ98)

The ios lane asked whether `position` places a member by a proposed day, so that no row moves when its member confirms (`F01-friends-first-days`: Ben · You, Maya, Alex, Sam, Debby, Nina, with Ben and Debby unconfirmed). Spec 09 asks for "stable roster order" and no reshuffling "after reactions"; handoff 6 has "Friends keeps stable roster order"; FQ26 reads it as the schedule's order from Monday. Nothing says what it is while members are still choosing. Default:

- **In the club's first days** (until the first-days proposal has applied), every member in the order they joined. A row moves neither when its member chooses nor when they confirm, and Friends never shows a proposed day, an abandoned one or a choice (`abandonedProposal`: "no previous owner in public views"). In the fixture the join order is Ben, Maya, Alex, Sam, Debby and Nina (`rosters.sundayPeople`), as `F01-friends-first-days` draws them.
- **Afterwards**, the schedule in force today, from Monday, a member with two days placed by the earlier (FQ26's rule, today's description); then anyone with no day in force (a membership change's new member until its start) in the order they joined.
- So rows move only when the first days end, on their last confirmation or on a leave that leaves only confirmed members (FQ102), which every member sees anyway as every day then has a date, and when a membership change applies, at its start (FQ81). Positions are 1 to the member count, each once; never a ranking (spec 09).
- The alternative, recorded in FQ98: place each member by the weekday the first-days proposal gave them, which keeps rows still at the end of the first days too, but shows the proposed week, an abandoned day's previous owner included, in Friends.

### 1.5 FQ99

In a club of two or three, whether a member whose one day is contested can confirm the other. Default: no; a member confirms both days at once (book p11; `G04-two-days-one-chosen`'s "Confirm two days"), so neither has a reveal until the clash ends, while every other member's confirmed days go ahead (FQ55). Confirming one day alone would change the confirmation's rule, `my_state` (which would then be per day) and FQ57's rule that a member chooses nothing after confirming (which would then apply per day).

### 1.6 Dates (FQ79, FQ101)

- **`ScheduleSlot.first_date`** is present only on a day confirmed in the first days (`taken`, or `yours` once the caller has confirmed), and is that day's first local date by FQ79: the first of its dates whose 7 AM, in the club's zone, is at least 24 elapsed hours after the confirmation; exactly 24 hours qualifies, and a clock change doesn't shorten them. It is absent on a day nobody has confirmed, which has no date yet (T49; handoff 5's check; handoff 6: "choosing an unconfirmed recurring assignment does not promise a dated occurrence"; `firstDays.noOccurrences`), and on every slot of a membership change. Today's description moves an unconfirmed day's date "as time passes", a reading with no source.
- **The first days' `effective_from`**, and `current.effective_from` while they last, is the club's first local date, fixed: never moved by a confirmation, and never an opening date, which each day has on its own (`first_date`).
- **`CurrentSchedule.assignments` takes one** (min 1, was 2): in the first days, each day goes ahead as its member confirms it (FQ55), so the days confirmed so far are the schedule in force while others still choose, as `db/migrations/0005_schedules_and_days.sql` already stores them. "Read-only in the trial" becomes: members change it only by confirming their first days or a membership change (FQ57).
- **A membership change's days:**
  - **Kept and announced:** every day already generated before `effective_from` stays as announced (spec 05, FQ61). **`ScheduleProposal.announced`** (new, optional, membership change only) lists those of them that haven't closed yet, as `Occurrence`s in date order, for `G04-change`'s "Current days through {the day before effective_from}" and its person groups of kept dates (`changeRows.kept`, `keptDates`: "Any count"). The app can't work them out itself without its own clock (T1) and without guessing at held days (FQ63). It is absent for the change's new member (`changeRows.MayaVariant`: "No pre-join preserved-occurrence block"). Kept weekdays then continue from `effective_from`.
  - **New:** a weekday new for its member, a day passing from another member included, is made at `effective_from` and first opens on its first date whose 7 AM is at least 24 elapsed hours after both the last confirmation (FQ79's X-14) and the start of `effective_from` in the club's zone (FQ101), so nothing about it shows before the start (FQ81). A new day on the start's own weekday therefore first opens a week later, with no reveal on that weekday in between (X-12). Its date then shows where dates show (`Member.next_day`, Home).
- `effective_from` changes only with a new proposal (FQ49).

### 1.7 FQ100, and what stays open

- **FQ100, a join in the first days once a day is confirmed** (FQ55's reading makes it a membership change, and the contract keeps one open proposal, `Schedule.proposal`). Default: the new member joins at once, with no day; the membership change that gives them one is proposed once the first-days proposal has applied. Until then the open proposal is the first days', which doesn't include them: they get only `current` from `getSchedule`, so a contested day shows to them as an unused one, not as Contested (FQ46 names it Contested only to members of the proposal), and `NOT_FOUND` from its proposal's operations. Nobody is moved, no winner is picked (T6, FQ55), and no unconfirmed member's holds are carried into a named proposed week, which would show members who have confirmed what `weekRows.unconfirmed` keeps from them. The cost: a clash that never ends leaves the new member without a day, and the new member sees a contested day as unused.
- **What stays open, for B2's schedule slice**, kept in `Schedule`'s description: how the worker's rolling generation meets a pending change's start (it holds off generating from `effective_from`, or the change is replaced once generation reaches it: FQ49, FQ61, FQ62), which must fit `schedule_versions_insert_guard` (`db/migrations/0005_schedules_and_days.sql`) and must not show, before `effective_from`, whether everyone has confirmed (FQ81, FQ101). The other gaps the description lists today close in this PR.

### 1.8 Who sees what (T55), `Schedule`'s description

`Schedule`'s "The same for every active member, except each one's own slots (T55)" becomes the explicit list:
- **a member choosing in the first days:** `current` (the days confirmed so far); each slot's state; `owner` and `first_date` on `taken`; `holders` on a day they hold with others; `held_by` on a day one other member holds;
- **a member who has confirmed in the first days:** the same, with `first_date` on their own days, and no `held_by` (they hold no contested day, so no `holders` either);
- **a member of a membership change, confirmed or not:** `current` (the schedule in force today); `yours` and `available` slots; `held_by` on every day the change proposes for another member; `announced`, unless they are its new member; no `first_date`, `owner` or `holders`; the same `state` and `version` until `effective_from`, whoever has confirmed;
- **a member the open proposal doesn't include** (FQ100): `current` only;
- **anyone without an active membership:** `NOT_FOUND`.

## 3. `text()` and `name()` (`packages/contracts/src/common.ts`)

- `.max(max * 16, { abort: true })`: a string past its guard is refused with one `too_big` issue, at its field's path, and nothing after the guard runs, the grapheme count, `name()`'s pattern and its visible-character refine included. The count then costs what a field's guard allows (about a millisecond for 8,000 code points that are each a cluster, measured on Node 26; a few milliseconds on Node 22), never what a body holds (259 ms for a megabyte today).
- `.refine((value) => value.isWellFormed(), { error: "well formed, with no lone surrogate", abort: true })` before the count: a lone surrogate is refused in every `name()` and `text()` field (`DisplayName`, `ClubName`, `CandidateLabel`, `Caption`, `CommentText`, `ReportNote`, `SearchText`), which Postgres would otherwise keep as U+FFFD. `String.prototype.isWellFormed` is Node 26's and TypeScript's `es2024` lib's. Each of those schemas' descriptions says "well formed (no lone surrogate)", and the `RULES` test of `contract.test.ts` asks for it.
- The doc comment: 16 code points a cluster (zod's `.max` and the document's `maxLength` count code points, not code units). `openapi.yaml` changes only in those descriptions.

## 4. The body limits (`tests/src/privacy/body-limits.test.ts`, `services/api/src/app.ts`)

- **The measure was wrong.** It charges six bytes for each unit of a string's `maxLength`, but `maxLength` counts code points, and JSON writes a code point outside the BMP as two escapes, twelve bytes. Corrected: `2 + 12 × maxLength`, except a string whose `pattern` holds it to ASCII (`^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$`, `^[0-9a-fA-F]+$` and the time zone's), which stays at six a unit; any other pattern takes the larger figure. Keys and enum values are ASCII, as before.
- **What it finds** (measured in the PR): `createReport`, `createComment` and `updateComment` over 90,000 bytes, past the 64 KiB `BODY_LIMIT`; `updateMe` past its 8 KiB. A valid `updateMe` body of over 8 KiB (every key and code unit escaped, a name of 640 code points) is refused unread today. `sendEvents` stays at 48,442: its strings are all ASCII, so the step's worry about its room is answered. `confirmScheduleDays`' body grows by its `weekdays`, a few dozen bytes.
- **The limits rise** to `BODY_LIMIT` 128 KiB and `updateMe` 17 KiB, the least whole number of KiB that keeps `updateMe`'s largest valid body (8,442 bytes) under half its limit, as `me.test.ts` asks on `main` ("under half the limit"), so that assertion stays as it is. They were the api lane's own choice in slice 8's review ("no source names a limit"), set to bound the grapheme count's cost after a failed guard, which section 3 removes; keeping them would mean keeping a measure known to be wrong (T58). `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`'s row of 2026-11-11 said "the limits stay"; the PR says why they don't, and closes the row. The pinned values move on purpose: `services/api/src/app.test.ts` (128 KiB and 17 KiB), the matrix's body past `updateMe`'s limit (`tests/src/privacy/account.ts`, 17 KiB, bounded in `account.test.ts`), and `me.test.ts`' largest name, now the astral worst case (40 clusters of 16 code points, 1,280 code units, every key escaped), which must reach the command, still under half the limit.
- A new `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` row: zod's `.max` and JSON Schema's `maxLength` count code points, and what that costs a body.
