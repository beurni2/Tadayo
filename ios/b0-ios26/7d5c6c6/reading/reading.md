# The reading of run A: iOS 27.2 against iOS 26.5 (`ios/b0-ios26`)

Run A is `ios` run 43496781835 at `7d5c6c6`, whose evidence is `ios/b0-ios26/7d5c6c6/ios/`. All 222 of its images, 214 `changed` and 8 `new`, were read against their iOS 26.5 references on `main` (`git show beb2435:<path>`), as `docs/plans/ios-b0-ios26.md` section 4.3 says. Run A drew every image the test suite asserts on iOS 27.2, the runtime the references now belong to; iOS 26.5 passed 119 of 119 tests without the snapshot tests.

## How it was read

- **Panels** (`panels/<state>.jpg` beside this file): the 26.5 reference, the 27.2 image, and the reference in grey with red wherever any channel differs by more than 8/255. Scaled to 1800 pixels tall at most; tall images were read in full-size crops.
- **Measurements**, made in the session with Pillow and NumPy (scripts in the session's scratchpad, not committed): for each state the rows that differ by more than 8/255, in points (the Bands column), the largest channel difference (Max), the canvas pixel, and every ink line (a run of rows holding a pixel more than 24/255 from the canvas) paired between the two images with its top, bottom, left and right, so that a line moved by one pixel shows.
- **Readers:** eight independent readers, one per family (Lab in three parts, sign-in in three, the tabs, the root), each opening every image's panel and full-size crops of every band and every moved line. A ninth reader, who had not seen the work, re-read all 80 images the readers flagged as a defect or unsure and a sample of 12 expected ones, trying to prove each verdict wrong. It agreed with every measurement and every verdict, and found the readers' classes inconsistent in the three places the decisions below settle.
- **SHA-256:** of the image read, as fetched from `evidence`. Commit B takes an image in only if its SHA-256 is still this one (the plan's section 4.2).

## Three kinds the plan's classes didn't name

The plan counts app content moved by a point or more as a defect (D2), except a change in the bar's height, and a changed shape the app draws as D1. Three differences meet those sizes, and each comes from the system's own drawing of a control or symbol the app uses unchanged. The app sets nothing that iOS 27.2 undoes in them (so none is D7), and the only way to hold the old positions would be to fix sizes the system now computes, by version, which T45 rules out. They are classed as the system's drawing, measured here and in INVENTORY's "The snapshots' phone":

1. **Leave sign-in and the title beside it (S1).** iOS 27.2 draws the bar's text action, the system's plain `Button` (`LeaveSignInButton`), heavier and wider: the label is 2.5 pt wider (3.5 pt at the largest text), and its capsule with it. The bar's title, which the system places, moves right by as much where it is too long to stay centred: "Your profile" on every A03 state (2.5 pt; 3.5 pt at the largest text), and "Sign in" on A02-age at the largest text (3.5 pt). Its size and its rows are unchanged.
2. **The tab items (S2).** Inside the tab bar the unselected labels move up to 1 pt left, and on F01 the selected symbol 1 pt.
3. **`star.fill` beside "Highlight" (S4).** The symbol is the same raster on both, but iOS 27.2 sets it higher on the word's baseline: 1 px higher at 3x at the default size, 3 px at the largest. In `HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline)` (`FoldedCard.highlightTag`, `PhotoHighlightLabel`) its box now reaches above the word's, so the row grows above the baseline:
    - the folded card with the author's own Highlight is 2 px (0.67 pt) taller at the default and xxxLarge sizes, at 393 and 430 points, and 5 px (1.67 pt) taller at the largest size, and everything under it moves by as much (`Lab-cards`, `Lab-card-highlight`, their siblings);
    - the Highlight label on a photograph is 1 pt taller at xxxLarge and 2 pt at the largest size, at its top edge only, with its padding unchanged (`Lab-revealed-large`, `-xl`, `-320-xl`).
    The word, its baseline and every gap the app sets are unchanged.

## The tab symbols (D7)

On iOS 27.2 the unselected tabs' symbols are drawn filled, in all 39 tab states, light, dark and at the largest text, although `TabsView` sets `\.symbolVariants` to `.none` for them and iOS 26.5 draws them outlined. The selected tab is still marked by the glass selection and `action`, and VoiceOver says it.

**The one bounded attempt.** Apple documents no way to choose an unselected tab symbol's variant: setting `\.symbolVariants` to `.none`, the documented opt-out, is what `TabsView` already does. A probe branch, `ios/b0-ios26-tabprobe` at `751f12d` (never merges), built the tabs with `Tab(value:content:label:)`, the documented replacement for `tabItem(_:)`, setting the variant on the `Label` for Home and Memories and on the icon's `Image` alone for Friends. Its `ios` run 43514132404 compiled, passed on iOS 26.5 (119 of 119 tests), and drew 221 of the 222 images byte-identical to run A's; the other, `H01-error-no-club`, differs by at most 17/255 in 17 pixels of the tab bar's glass. Every unselected symbol is still filled. So the tab symbols are a Known difference, and their 27.2 references are taken as drawn: a `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` row and the needs list's item 60 record it, and INVENTORY's tab-bar bullet, `TabsView`'s docstring and the T45 audit row say what each iOS draws.

> **Correction (2026-11-14, PR #57's review):** 17 is the count of pixels that differ by more than 8/255. The whole difference is 9,038 pixels of the tab bar's glass, at most 17/255: the second way iOS 27.2 draws that glass (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`, the row of 2026-11-14).
